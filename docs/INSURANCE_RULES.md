# INSURANCE OPPORTUNITY INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
## RADAR BUSINESS ROMÂNIA — Deterministic Rules Engine & Insurance Taxonomy

### 1. Philosophy & Ground Rules

1. **Deterministic & Explainable**: Every insurance recommendation is generated by auditable business logic based strictly on confirmed company attributes (CAEN activity, asset footprint, revenue scale, headcount, public tenders, and legal form).
2. **Fact vs. Inference Separation**: The engine never fabricates that a company owns a fleet or operates a hotel unless verified by CAEN codes, balance sheets, or official data.
3. **Broker Prospecting Rationale**: Recommendations provide concrete sales talking points, legal obligations under Romanian legislation, and questions for broker discovery calls.

---

### 2. Insurance Rule Catalog

#### Rule Set 1: Construction & Civil Works
- **Product**: `CAR (Constructor's All Risks) & Contractor's Third-Party Liability`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `4120`, `4211`, `4212`, `4213`, `4221`, `4222`, `4291`, `4299`, `4399`.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Company registered for building/infrastructure construction; revenue/asset growth.
  - **Rationale**: Mandatory for major municipal and private developer contracts in Romania under Law 10/1995 on construction quality. Covers damage to works in progress, machinery, and third-party property/injuries.
  - **Additional Information Needed**: Current active work sites, contract values, project duration, subcontractor reliance.
  - **Confidence**: 0.95.

- **Product**: `Performance & Bid Bonds (Garanții de Bună Execuție / Participare SEAP)`
  - **Triggers**: Construction/IT/Services companies winning public procurement tenders or registered in public contracting.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Public procurement participation, capitalization.
  - **Rationale**: Financial guarantee letter replacing cash collateral deposits held by state contracting authorities.
  - **Confidence**: 0.90.

#### Rule Set 2: Transport, Freight & Logistics
- **Product**: `CMR Carrier's Liability (Răspunderea Cărăușului Internațional / Național)`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `4941` (Freight transport by road), `4942` (Removal services), `5229` (Freight forwarding).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Transportation activity code, ARR license prerequisite.
  - **Rationale**: Mandatory protection under the CMR Convention (Geneva 1956) for damage or loss to goods in transit. Freight forwarders require Forwarder's Liability (Răspundere Expeditor).
  - **Additional Information Needed**: Number of registered trucks, international vs. domestic routes, typical high-value cargo types.
  - **Confidence**: 0.95.

- **Product**: `Commercial Motor Fleet (CASCO Flotă & RCA)`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `4941`, `4931`, `4932` (Taxi/Ride-hailing), `7711` (Car rental), `5320` (Courier).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Motor operations as primary business model.
  - **Rationale**: Fleet discount structures, driver accident rider, road assistance 24/7 across Europe.
  - **Confidence**: 0.90.

#### Rule Set 3: Technology, Software & Telecom
- **Product**: `Tech Professional Indemnity (E&O - Errors & Omissions)`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `6201`, `6202`, `6203`, `6209`, `5829`.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Software development or IT consulting operations.
  - **Rationale**: Standard contractual requirement by EU/US clients to protect against system downtime, data corruption, failed software delivery, or IP infringement.
  - **Additional Information Needed**: Client geography (US/Canada vs. EU jurisdiction), average contract size, code audit procedures.
  - **Confidence**: 0.92.

- **Product**: `Cyber Risk & Data Breach Liability`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `6201`, `6311` (Data processing/hosting), `4791` (E-Commerce), `6419` (Fintech/Finance).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Data processing, SaaS, or high-volume online consumer transactions.
  - **Rationale**: Covers GDPR regulatory defense costs, forensic investigation, extortion/ransomware negotiation, and customer notification expenses.
  - **Confidence**: 0.88.

#### Rule Set 4: Hospitality & Tourism
- **Product**: `Hotel & Restaurant Commercial Property & Business Interruption`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `5510` (Hotels), `5520` (Holiday accommodation), `5610` (Restaurants), `5621` (Catering).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Accommodation or food service registered entity.
  - **Rationale**: Comprehensive protection against fire, earthquake, pipe burst, kitchen explosion, and resulting revenue loss during repair shutdowns.
  - **Additional Information Needed**: Building ownership vs. lease agreement, square meters, seating capacity, ISU fire permit status.
  - **Confidence**: 0.92.

- **Product**: `Public Liability & Food Poisoning Coverage`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `5610`, `5621`, `5630`, `5510`.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Customer-facing premises with active physical patronage.
  - **Rationale**: Protection against slips and falls, customer property damage, and food contamination claims.
  - **Confidence**: 0.88.

#### Rule Set 5: Healthcare & Medical
- **Product**: `Medical Malpractice (Răspundere Civilă Medicală)`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `8610` (Hospital activities), `8621` (General medical), `8622` (Specialist medical), `8623` (Dental practice).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Official healthcare provider status.
  - **Rationale**: Mandatory legal obligation under Romanian Law 95/2006 for all healthcare practitioners and medical clinic operating licenses.
  - **Confidence**: 0.98.

- **Product**: `High-Value Medical Equipment Breakdown`
  - **Triggers**: CAEN `8610`, `8622`, `8690` (Diagnostics, MRI, CT, dental units).
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Capital-intensive medical apparatus.
  - **Rationale**: Covers sudden electrical or mechanical breakdown, operator error, power surges for MRI/CT/ultrasound machines.
  - **Confidence**: 0.85.

#### Rule Set 6: Corporate Governance & Workforce
- **Product**: `Directors & Officers Liability (D&O)`
  - **Triggers**: SA legal form, large/medium enterprises (Turnover > 10M RON or Employees > 20), or IT/Venture-backed entities.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Corporate structure, substantial turnover or investor participation.
  - **Rationale**: Protects personal personal assets of administrators, managers, and board members from shareholder or third-party lawsuits alleging wrongful management acts.
  - **Confidence**: 0.85.

- **Product**: `Group Employee Health & Accidental Death/Disability`
  - **Triggers**: Companies with verified headcount ≥ 10 employees.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Confirmed average number of employees from financial statements.
  - **Rationale**: Essential talent retention benefit, tax-deductible up to €400/employee/year under Romanian Fiscal Code Art. 76(4).
  - **Confidence**: 0.89.
